Case Study

Safeguarding Cloud Inboxes & Preventing Data Leaks via Advanced Email Defense

Client Profile
A fast-growing financial institution and corporate departments handling high volumes of sensitive financial communications.
Partner
I.T. Solutions India Pvt Ltd
Technology Used
Sophos Email Security (Integrated Cloud Email Security & DLP)

Executive Summary

To protect business inboxes from targeted phishing, Business Email Compromise (BEC), and accidental data leaks, I.T. Solutions India Pvt Ltd deployed Sophos Email Security. The deployment secured cloud email platforms, prevented credential theft, and automated outbound data loss prevention.

The Challenge

  • Evasive Phishing & Impersonation: High exposure to sophisticated phishing, executive impersonation, and malicious link attacks.
  • Data Exfiltration Risks: Strict outbound content controls were needed to stop accidental leaks of sensitive financial data and customer PII.
  • In-Box Threat Exposure: The client needed automatic retraction of post-delivery threats that bypass initial perimeter gateway filters.

The Solution

  • AI-Powered BEC & Phishing Defense: Applied NLP and sender authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) to detect spoofing and impersonation attempts.
  • Automated Post-Delivery Clawback: Removed malicious emails from user inboxes post-delivery when weaponized links were detected.
  • Outbound DLP & Encryption: Enforced multi-rule DLP policies to scan outgoing mail and apply policy-based encryption.
  • Cloud Sandboxing & Time-of-Click Protection: Scanned attachments in an AI-powered sandbox and rewrote malicious URLs to block zero-day threats in real time.

Key Outcomes & Impact

  • 99%+ Phishing Risk Reduction: Blocked malware-free impersonation attacks and credential phishing before user interaction.
  • Zero Data Exfiltration: Supported compliance and data privacy with automated outbound message encryption.
  • Zero Disruption Deployment: Connected via API without altering core DNS or mail-flow configurations.
  • Proactive Post-Delivery Protection: Removed malicious emails automatically using real-time threat intelligence updates.

Results

Protected 1,200+ mailboxes with Sophos Email Security, filtering out thousands of malicious emails monthly. Reduced email-borne phishing threats by 85% and cut spam volume targeting executive teams by over 90%.

Key outcomeBy pairing Sophos Email Security with our tailored deployment strategy, I.T. Solutions India Pvt Ltd shields critical corporate communications from advanced phishing threats while keeping sensitive data secure.
