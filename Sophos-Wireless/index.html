Case Study

Upgrading Campus & Business Security with Sophos Wireless Access Points

Client / Sector
Multi-site enterprise, higher education, and retail environments.
Challenge
Slow Wi-Fi speeds, network congestion, unmanaged guest access, and disjointed security controls between endpoints and wireless access.
Solution
Sophos AP6 Series Wireless Access Points with Wi-Fi 6 / 6E, managed centrally through Sophos Central.

The Challenge

The client faced frequent network dropouts during peak hours and lacked visibility over unauthorized devices connecting to Wi-Fi. Managing separate consoles for firewalls, switches, and access points created administrative overhead and delayed response when security threats occurred.

The Solution

  • Unified Cloud Management: Centralized control of all access points via Sophos Central alongside existing firewalls and switches.
  • Next-Gen Speed & Reliability: Wi-Fi 6 / Wi-Fi 6E with 2.5G multi-gigabit Ethernet interfaces helped eliminate bandwidth bottlenecks.
  • Active Threat Response: Automated isolation of compromised wireless devices helped prevent lateral malware movement.
  • Secure Guest Wi-Fi: Easy-to-configure captive portals with isolated network segments for visitors.

Key Results & Impact

  • 50% Reduction: Less time spent on routine network administration and troubleshooting.
  • 4x Network Capacity: Faster performance with low-latency connectivity in high-density areas.
  • Instant Threat Isolation: Automatic containment of infected devices at the access layer.
  • Single-Pane Management: Total visibility over users, SSIDs, and application bandwidth.

Results

Deployed Sophos Access Points across 5 campus facilities, seamlessly covering over 1,500 daily active wireless clients. Reduced Wi-Fi dead zones to zero and cut guest onboarding and connectivity support tickets by 65%.

Key outcomeIntegrating Sophos Access Points into our central platform gave us total visibility. We shifted from daily troubleshooting to proactive management overnight.
