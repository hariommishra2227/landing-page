Case Study

Optimizing LAN Access, Power & Port Security via Sophos Switches

Client Profile
A fast-growing financial institution and corporate departments expanding local network access.
Partner
I.T. Solutions India Pvt Ltd
Technology Used
Sophos Switch Series (Cloud-Managed Access Layer Switches)

Executive Summary

I.T. Solutions India Pvt Ltd deployed Sophos Switches to deliver secure, high-speed LAN connectivity, power edge devices via Power-over-Ethernet (PoE), and enforce access layer security across client branches.

Key Highlights

  • Challenge: Unsecured Ethernet ports, network traffic bottlenecks, and lack of port-level visibility across multi-site offices.
  • Cloud Management: Single-pane-of-glass switch management and zero-touch configuration via Sophos Central.
  • Power & Performance: Multi-gigabit speeds with PoE+ support to power IP phones, cameras, and Wi-Fi 6 Access Points.
  • Access Security: Strict VLAN segmentation and port security to isolate untrusted IoT devices and prevent lateral threat movement.
  • Active Threat Response: Instant port isolation when combined with Sophos Endpoint to contain infected devices automatically.

Results

Deployed Sophos Switches across 8 office locations and 350+ network ports to unify LAN infrastructure. Reduced local network latency by 40% and cut port provisioning and management time from 2 days to under 2 hours.

Key outcomeBy integrating Sophos Switches at the LAN edge, I.T. Solutions India Pvt Ltd ensures seamless device connectivity and unified security control across every corporate port.
